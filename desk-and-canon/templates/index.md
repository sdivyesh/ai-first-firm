## Folders

- Root: firm-level reference pages. Few, stable, reviewed on a date.
- `assets/`: finished deliverables, case studies, research. Filed as made; replaced, never edited in place.
- `kb/`: pages about things built: how they work, what they cost, what was rolled back.

## Conventions

- Plain text, Markdown. No binaries; a binary lives on the Desk and the Canon holds a page about it.
- A page is replaced whole; the repository holds the history.
- "Last reviewed" is the date a person last said the page still stands.
